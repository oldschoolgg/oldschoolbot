Yama is a killable boss. To start a trip, use [[/k name\:Yama]].

The generated [Monsters](/osb/monsters/#yama) page lists Yama's current costs, requirements, and kill boosts.

## Progression

Yama drops [[Dossier]], [[Forgotten lockbox]], [[Oathplate shards]], [[Soulflame horn]], Oathplate pieces, and [[Yami]].

[[Dossier]] and [[Forgotten lockbox]] can be opened with [[/open]]. Dossier has dry protection for [[Rite of vile transference]] at 100 Yama KC. After the rite is already owned or used, duplicate rite rolls are converted into [[Chasm teleport scroll]] instead.

Use [[/use item\:Rite of vile transference]] to unlock its PvM boosts.

## Rite of Vile Transference

Rite of Vile Transference boosts the following monsters:

- Yama: 5%
- Scurrius: 5%
- Amoxliatl: 3%
- Royal Titans: 5%
- Nightmare: 5%
- Phosani's Nightmare: 5%
- Grotesque Guardians: 5%
- Cerberus: 5%
- Araxxor: 7%
- Hydra: 3%
- Alchemical Hydra: 3%

Contract Yama fights are not currently implemented.

## Yama Smithables

Yama adds smithing recipes for [[Infernal nugget]], [[Infernal chunk]], [[Infernal plate]], [[Oathplate helm]], [[Oathplate chest]], and [[Oathplate legs]]. These are made with [[/smith]] and require [[smithing:83]].

[[Infernal shale]] can be mined with [[/mine name\:Infernal shale]] and currently produces [[Crushed infernal shale]].
