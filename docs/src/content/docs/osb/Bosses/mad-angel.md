The Mad Angel is a killable boss found in Ardeaglais, the cathedral on Wyrmscraig. To start a trip, use [[/k name\:Mad Angel]].

The generated [Monsters](/osb/monsters/#mad-angel) page lists the Mad Angel's current costs, requirements, and kill boosts.

## Requirements

- Completion of **Fallen From Grace** ([[/activities quest name\:Fallen From Grace]]), which requires [[crafting:60]], [[mining:53]] and [[runecraft:47]].
- [[attack:60]], [[strength:60]] and [[prayer:43]] (for Protect from Melee and Protect from Magic).

In-game, Fallen From Grace also requires 62 Sailing and the quest Pandemonium. These are not required in the bot.

## Strategy

The Mad Angel is a golem and is weak to crush. A melee setup is used, and the [[Granite hammer]] gives the largest boost because of its golembane effect. Each kill uses about 0.5 [[Prayer potion(4)]] and 0.25 [[Super combat potion(4)]].

## Drops

Every kill drops 25-35 [[Granite dust]]. Notable drops:

- [[Ardeaglais teleport]] (2): 1/25
- [[Hallowfell]]: 1/127. A two-handed sword that requires [[attack:75]].
- [[Jar of Light]]: 1/1000
- [[Aggy]] (pet): 1/2000
- [[Clue scroll (medium)]]: 1/25
