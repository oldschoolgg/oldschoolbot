The Verdant Island features a tiered hierarchy of bosses ranging from mid-game crystal encounters to high-level dragon fights, the **Burning Dominion** mass, and the elusive **Archon**.

---

## Solo Boss Encounters

### Crystalline Sentinel

The entry-level Verdant Island boss, fought with melee and ranged combat styles.

- **Command:** [[/k name:Crystalline Sentinel]]
- **Requirements:** [[attack:70]] [[strength:70]] [[defence:70]] [[hitpoints:70]]
- **Minimum Gear:** Rune armour, Dragon scimitar, Dragon defender
- **QP Required:** 500 | **Kill Time:** ~7 minutes
- **Supplies per Kill:** 1x Super combat potion(4), 2x Prayer potion(4)
- **Speed Boosts:**
  - Vitrolic curse (melee): +25%
  - Elderflame bow + Elderflame arrows: +10%
  - Brawler's hook necklace (melee): +10%
  - Starfire bow + Elderflame arrows: +7%
- **Notable Drops:** Sentinel core (1/1000), Starfire bow (u) (1/3000), Celestyte, Oneiryte, Starfire agate, Crystalline ore, Dense crystal shard, Crystalline plank, Gem infused ore, Elderflame arrowtips.

## The Burning Dominion (Mass Raid)

The **Burning Dominion** is a high-intensity 2–10 player mass encounter where you face both Orym and Orrodil simultaneously.

- **Command:** [[/k name:Burning Dominion mass]]
- **Party Size:** 2 to 10 players.
- **Base Kill Time:** ~10 minutes per kill (reduced by team size and gear boosts).

### Strict Gear & Entry Requirements
All participants must meet these strict criteria to start or join the mass:
1. **Dragonbane Glaive:** Must be equipped in the `melee` gear setup.
2. **Dragonbane Aegis:** Must be equipped in the `melee` gear setup.
3. **Combatant's Cape:** Must be owned / equipped.
4. **Maxed Igne Tame:** All players must own a maxed Igne Tame.
5. **Combat Levels:** [[attack:115]] [[strength:115]] [[defence:115]] [[magic:115]] [[ranged:115]] [[hitpoints:115]] [[slayer:115]]
6. **Ironman Allowed:** Like Ignecarus, Ironmen are permitted to host and join Burning Dominion mass parties.

### Consumables per Kill
Each participant consumes the following items per kill upon trip start:
- **1.5x Brimstone elixir** (minimum 2x)
- **3x Enhanced saradomin brew**
- **1x Enhanced super restore**
- **1x Super combat potion(4)**
- **1x Enhanced stamina potion**

> **Supply Failure Mechanic:** Bringing standard Saradomin brews or Super restores instead of their Enhanced variants will result in death in every kc of a trip.

### Speed Boosts
- **Empyrean Great Set:** +10% speed per piece (+50% for full set)
- **Searcrown band:** +7% speed

### Loot & Uniques
Combined, enhanced drop tables rolling uniques from both Orym and Orrodil at improved rates, including **Forsaken tear**, **Sacrilegious flask**, **Shattered pendant**, **Celestial flame**, **Elder scroll pieces**, **Elder sigil fragments**, and both pets (**Orylin** & **Orrodin**).

## The Archon (Megaboss)

The **Archon** is an elusive encounter with a **1-in-20 chance** to spawn after completing any eligible Verdant Island trip.

### Spawn Requirements
- **Archon Sanctum Tier 1+** must be built and currently maintained.
- **Trip Duration:** Trip must be at least **30 minutes** in length.
- Eligible activities: all Verdant Island monsters, Brimstone Distillery, Construction Contracts, Ancient Mycology, Archaic Mining, and Gemstone Fishing.
- When an event triggers, you will receive a button prompt to fight the Archon.

### Archon Tiers

| Tier | Name | Milestone Condition | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Shrouded Archon** | Level 120 in any combat skill (104.3M XP) | ~5 minutes |
| **Tier 2** | **Ascendant Archon** | 1 Billion XP in 2+ combat skills | ~10 minutes |
| **Tier 3** | **Apotheotic Archon** | 5 Billion XP in 3+ combat skills | ~20 minutes |

### Elderflame Arrow Requirements
The Archon demands **Elderflame arrows equipped directly in your ranged ammo slot**:

| Tier | Equipped Arrows (With Saver) | Equipped Arrows (Without Saver) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **50** arrows | **150** arrows |
| **Tier 2** | **100** arrows | **300** arrows |
| **Tier 3** | **200** arrows | **600** arrows |

- **Arrow Savers:** Equipping a **Tidal collector (i)** or **Combatant's cape** in the range cape slot reduces arrow consumption by **3x**.
- Arrows are deducted directly from the equipped ammo slot upon launching the encounter.

### Gear Score Calculation
Archon kill speed scales with a weighted composite gear score evaluated across all 3 combat setups:
- **Ranged Setup (50% weight):** Elderflame bow, Elderflame arrows, Tidal collector (i), Farsight snapshot necklace, Ring of piercing (i), Gorajan archer set.
- **Melee Setup (25% weight):** Axe of the high sungod, Empyrean armour set, Brawler's hook necklace, Searcrown band, TzKal cape, Vitrolic curse.
- **Mage Setup (25% weight):** Void staff, Abyssal tome, Arcane blast necklace, Spellbound ring (i), Vasa cloak, Gorajan occult set.

| Reward | Tier 1 Rate | Tier 2 Rate | Tier 3 Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Archibald (Pet)** | **1/7,000** | **1/5,000** | **1/3,000** |
| **Prismare ring (u)** | 1/600 | 1/400 | **1/200** |
| **Empyrean shards** | 2–5 | 4–10 | **6–15** (+bonus rolls) |
| **Elder scroll piece** | 1/100 | 1/75 | **1/50** |
| **Elder sigil fragment (1–3)** | 1/50 | 1/35 | **1/20** |
| **Coins** | 50k–200k | 500k–2M | **5M–20M** |
| **Elder logs / runes / planks** | Moderate | High | Massive |

- **Archon Relic:** Obtained from Elder Caches. Using `/use item:Archon relic` consumes the relic and immediately summons an Archon encounter on demand.
- **Archibald Perks:** While equipped, **doubles all loot received from Archon encounters** (after gear reductions), and increases the Archon spawn chance on 30m+ island trips from 1/15 to **1/10**.

Archon Sanctum upgrades provide up to **+50% regular loot** and up to **+100% unique chance** at Tier 5.
