The Verdant Island allows you to make various pieces of unique gear by combining rare drops from Orym, Orrodil, the Burning Dominion, and the Archon.

---

## Empyrean Armour

The **Empyrean Armour set** is a direct upgrade to Gorajan warrior armour consisting of five pieces: **Greathelm**, **Greatplate**, **Greatgreaves**, **Greatgauntlets**, and **Greatsabaton**.

- **Set Effect:** Provides **+10% speed per piece** (+50% total) when fighting the **Burning Dominion**.
- **Base Requirement:** Any variant of the Gorajan warrior set (standard, primal, oceanic) can be used as the base.
- **Skills Required:** [[smithing:120]] [[crafting:120]]

### Full Set Crafting Recipe
- **1x Gorajan warrior set** (any variant)
- **750x Empyrean shards**
- **20x Dwarven bar**
- **10x Gorajan shards**
- **5x Elderflame catalyst**
- **2x Sun-metal bar**
- **5,000x Dark animica**

## Dragonbane Weaponry

### Dragonbane Glaive

The BIS stab weapon for dragon encounters, providing **+25% speed** across **all dragon monsters**, and required to participate in the **Burning Dominion**.

- **Command:** [[/create item:Dragonbane glaive]]
- **Skills Required:** [[smithing:120]] [[crafting:120]]
- **Dragon Boost:** +25% speed against all dragons (upgrades over Dragon hunter lance's +15%)

**Recipe:**
- **1x Dragon hunter lance**
- **1x Primordial spine**
- **1x Primordial heartstring**
- **2,500x Primordial scales**
- **3x Dwarven bar**
- **7,500x Dark animica**

---

### Dragonbane Aegis

The premier anti-dragon shield, providing complete dragonfire protection and **+15% speed** across **all dragon monsters** (stacking with the Glaive for +40% speed total), and strictly required to participate in the **Burning Dominion**.

- **Command:** [[/create item:Dragonbane aegis]]
- **Skills Required:** [[smithing:120]] [[crafting:120]]
- **Dragon Boost:** +15% speed against all dragons (and counts as Dragonfire shield/ward/Anti-dragon shield)

**Recipe:**
- **1x Dragonfire shield**
- **1x Dragonfire ward**
- **1x Wyvern shield**
- **1x Primordial heartstring**
- **2,500x Primordial scales**
- **3x Dwarven bar**
- **7,500x Dark animica**

## Accessories & Relics

### Celestial Pendant

A versatile utility amulet providing **+10% faster distillation** at the Brimstone Distillery and **+25% faster construction** at Construction Contracts. It also speeds up Fist of Guthix, Monkey Rumble, Stealing Creation, Tinkering Workshop, Baxtorian Bathhouses, ODS, Barbarian Assault, Shades of Morton, and Chompy Hunting.

- **Command:** [[/create item:Celestial pendant]]
- **Skills Required:** [[smithing:120]] [[crafting:120]]

**Recipe:**
- **1x Shattered pendant**
- **1x Primordial spine**
- **1x Primordial heartstring**
- **1x Dwarven bar**
- **7,500x Dark animica**

---

### Vitrolic Curse

An enhanced spiritual blessing functioning as an upgraded Dwarven Blessing that consumes **Brimstone Elixir** instead of prayer potions. Provides a **+25% speed boost** (+30% with Amulet of Zealots) across various bosses.

- **Command:** [[/create item:Vitrolic curse]]
- **Skill Required:** [[prayer:120]]
- **Consumption Rate:** 1x Brimstone elixir per 3 minutes of trip duration.
- **Prayer Master Cape:** Reduces consumption by **75%** (~1 to 3 elixirs per 30–45 minute trip).

**Recipe:**
- **1x Dwarven blessing**
- **1x Infernal core**
- **1x Sacrilegious flask**

---

### Searcrown Band

A BIS melee ring granting a **+7% speed bonus** against Orym, Orrodil, and the Burning Dominion.

- **Command:** [[/create item:Searcrown band]]

**Recipe:**
- **1x Ignis ring (i)**
- **1x Forsaken tear**
- **1x Dwarven bar**

## Other Gear

### Prismare Ring

An upgraded First Age Ring providing enhanced passive XP bonuses.

- **Creation:** Combine `1x Prismare ring (u)` + `1x First age ring` + `1x Elderflame catalyst` + `50x Empyrean shards`.
- **Reversion:** Run [[/create item:Revert prismare ring]] to separate the ring back into a `Prismare ring (u)` and `First age ring`.
- **Dismantle:** Can be dismantled for `250x Prismare` + `250x Empyrean shards`.

---

### Starfire Bow & Elderflame Bow

- **Starfire Bow (Fletching 95):**
  - **Recipe:** `1x Starfire bow (u)` + `1x Prolific twine`.
  - **Effect:** +7% speed at Sentinel, Behemoth, Orym, Orrodil, and the Elder Mimic with Elderflame arrows.
- **Elderflame Bow (Fletching 110):**
  - **Recipe:** `1x Starfire bow` + `1x Elderflame catalyst`.
  - **Effect:** +10% speed at Sentinel, Behemoth, Orym, Orrodil, and the Elder Mimic; primary weapon for Archon encounters.

---

### Elder Sigil & Scrolls

- **Elder Sigil:** Created by combining `Elder sigil fragment (1)`, `(2)`, and `(3)`.
- **Sources:** High-tier Construction Contracts, Orrodil, Burning Dominion, and the Archon.
