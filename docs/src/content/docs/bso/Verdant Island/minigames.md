---

## Brimstone Distillery

The **Brimstone Distillery** is a Herblore minigame where you distill potions and elixirs using coal, vials, catalysts, and various ingredients.

- **Command:** [[/activities brimstone_distillery]]
- **Level Requirement:** [[herblore:110]] (recipes scale up to level 120)
- **Primary Secondary:** Distillation produces **Diluted brimstone**, a critical reagent for high-tier upgrades and contracts.

### Quality Rolls & Distillation Events
Each distillation action rolls a quality score (0–100):
- **Disaster:** Severe failure resulting in ruined batches and lost output.
- **Normal:** Standard output of 1–2 potions.
- **Jackpot:** Extraordinary distillation yielding extra potions and bonus Diluted brimstone.
- **Pet:** Rare chance to roll the **Sedryn** spirit fox pet on trip completion.

### Distillation Boosts
- **Celestial pendant:** +10% faster distillation
- **Enhanced stamina potion:** +10% faster distillation (consumed)
- **Dragon's fury:** +10% faster distillation (consumed)
- **Herblore master cape:** 10% less coal & catalyst consumption
- **Full Graceful:** Prevents disastrous rolls (minimum quality floor of 20)
- **Settlement Infrastructure:** Up to +25% speed and rewards (+5% per tier)

## Construction Contracts

The **Construction Contracts** minigame allows you to fulfill orders across the island.

- **Command:** [[/activities construction_contracts]]
- **Level Requirement:** [[construction:110]] (contracts scale up to level 120)
- **Required Tools:** Saw (or Drygore saw / Crystal saw) and Hammer (or Dwarven greathammer)

### Scoring & Rewards
- Contracts scoring **30+ quality** are deemed successful.
- Rewards include **Gemstone satchels**, **Gemstone cores**, **Brimstone elixirs**, exotic seeds (Ignilace, Athelas, Korulsi), and rare rolls on **Elder scroll pieces** and **Elder sigil fragments**.
- **Pet:** Rare chance to roll the **Bamyr** deer pet on trip completion.

### Construction Boosts
- **Drygore saw:** +40% faster
- **Celestial pendant:** +25% faster
- **Construction master cape:** +15% faster
- **Full Carpenter's outfit:** +10% faster
- **Enhanced stamina potion:** +10% faster (consumed)
- **Dwarven greathammer:** +10% faster
- **Settlement Infrastructure:** Up to +25% speed and rewards (+5% per tier)
