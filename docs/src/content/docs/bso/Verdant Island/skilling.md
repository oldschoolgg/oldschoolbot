## Ancient Mycology (Woodcutting)

Harvest ancient myconid growths across the Island. You can optionally target a specific growth tier to grind targeted materials, or omit the option to harvest your highest unlocked tier automatically.

- **Command:** [[/activities ancient_mycology]]
- **Targeting Specific Tiers:** [[/activities ancient_mycology growth:[Growth Name]]] (e.g. `growth:Verdant logs`)
- **Level Requirement:** [[woodcutting:95]]
- **Speed Boosts:**
  - **Expedition Outfitters:** Up to +25% faster gathering (+5% per tier)
  - **Drygore saw:** +10% faster chopping
- **Woodcutting Master Cape:** Doubles all harvested logs/growths (~1.5k–2.5k logs/hr).

| Growth | Level | XP | Harvest Time | Notable Uses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verdant logs** | 95 | 250 | 2.2s | Verdant planks, Facilities |
| **Ancient cap** | 100 | 350 | 2.5s | Warcamp, Sanctum upgrades |
| **Colossal stem** | 105 | 480 | 2.8s | Warcamp, Grand Conduit |
| **Living bark** | 110 | 650 | 3.2s | Lumberyard, Conduit, Contracts |
| **Ancient verdant logs** | 115 | 900 | 3.8s | Ancient planks, Facilities, Conduit |

## Gemstone Fishing (Fishing)

Catch gemstone-bearing scaled fish inhabiting the deep island reefs.

- **Command:** [[/activities gemstone_fishing]]
- **Breakdown Command:** [[/activities gemstone_fishing action:breakdown]] or [[/open name:[Gemscale Name]]]
- **Level Requirement:** [[fishing:90]]
- **Speed Boosts:**
  - **Expedition Outfitters:** Up to +25% faster gathering (+5% per tier)
  - **Angler outfit:** Up to +2.5% bonus Fishing XP
- **Fishing Master Cape:** Doubles all caught gemscales (~1.5k–2.5k gemscales/hr).

| Catch Tier | Scaling Level | XP | Catch Time | Breakdown Yield |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Juvenile gemscale** | 90 | 250 | 2.2s | 2–4 Exotic Gems |
| **Adolescent gemscale** | 95 | 350 | 2.5s | 4–7 Exotic Gems |
| **Mature gemscale** | 100 | 480 | 2.8s | 6–10 Exotic Gems |
| **Ancient gemscale** | 105 | 650 | 3.2s | 10–16 Exotic Gems |
| **Elder gemscale** | 110 | 900 | 3.8s | 15–25 Exotic Gems |

*All gemscales have a 1/50 tertiary chance to yield **Prismare** upon breakdown/opening.*

## Archaic Mining (Mining)

The island features two specialized mining options via [[/activities archaic_mining]].

### 1. Dragonbone Mining
Mine colossal dragon remains. In addition to Mining XP, every action awards **50% bonus Prayer XP**.

- **Command:** [[/activities archaic_mining type:Dragonbone]]
- **Level Requirement:** [[mining:90]]
- **Pickaxe Requirement:** Requires an equipped pickaxe (Volcanic: +25% speed, Dwarven: +20% speed, Crystal: +10% speed, Dragon: +6% speed).
- **Offhand Volcanic Pickaxe:** +15% faster mining (requires 100 Strength & 105 Mining).
- **Expedition Outfitters:** Up to +25% faster gathering (+5% per tier).
- **Uniques:** 1/200,000 chance per action to find **Primordial heartstrings** or **Primordial spines**.
- **Mining Master Cape:** Doubles all mined bone outputs.

| Bone Deposit | Level | Mining XP | Bonus Prayer XP | Mine Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dragon bones** | 90 | 180 | 90 | 2.2s |
| **Superior dragon bones** | 95 | 260 | 130 | 2.5s |
| **Abyssal dragon bones** | 100 | 360 | 180 | 2.8s |
| **Frost dragon bones** | 105 | 480 | 240 | 3.1s |
| **Royal dragon bones** | 108 | 600 | 300 | 3.4s |
| **Primordial bones** | 110 | 750 | 375 | 3.8s |

### 2. Crystalline Mining
Mine veins of crystal. Each action rolls on the Island Gem Table.

- **Command:** [[/activities archaic_mining type:Crystalline]]
- **Level Requirement:** [[mining:90]]
- **Pickaxe Requirement:** Requires an equipped pickaxe (Volcanic: +25% speed, Dwarven: +20% speed, Crystal: +10% speed, Dragon: +6% speed).
- **Offhand Volcanic Pickaxe:** +15% faster mining (requires 100 Strength & 105 Mining).
- **Expedition Outfitters:** Up to +25% faster gathering (+5% per tier).
- **Mining Master Cape:** Doubles all mined crystal outputs:
  - **Crystalline ore:** ~2.5k/hr
  - **Gem infused ore:** ~2.0k/hr
  - **Dense crystal shard:** ~1.5k/hr

| Ore Deposit | Level | Mining XP | Mine Time | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crystalline ore** | 90 | 220 | 2.2s | Crystalline ore (3–5) |
| **Gem Infused ore** | 100 | 340 | 2.8s | Gem infused ore (3–5) |
| **Dense Crystal shard** | 110 | 520 | 3.8s | Dense crystal shard (3–5) |

### The Island Gem Table
Activities across the island roll on the Island Gem Table, awarding:
- **Exotic Gems (2–6 each):** Celestyte, Starfire agate, Verdantyte, Oneiryte, Firaxyte.
- **Tertiary Container Chances:**
  - Gemstone bundle: 1/25
  - Gemstone satchel: 1/75
  - Gemstone core: 1/150
  - Prismare: 1/500
