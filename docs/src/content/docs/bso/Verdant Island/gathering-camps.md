---
title: "Passive Gathering Camps"
---

The Verdant Island features **five passive resource gathering camps** that accumulate skilling materials around the clock without requiring minion trip time.

---

## Gathering Mechanics

- **Tick Rate:** Camps generate materials every **15 minutes** (96 ticks per 24 hours).
- **Storage Cap:** Accumulates up to **24 hours** of output before stopping.
- **Rolls per Tick:**
  - **T1:** 2 rolls
  - **T2:** 4 rolls
  - **T3:** 7 rolls
  - **T4:** 11 rolls
  - **T5:** 16 rolls
- **Output Multiplier:** 3.0x base quantity scaling (~80,640 items per week across camps at T5).
- **Skill Level Tiers:**
  - **T1:** Unlocks resources up to level 40.
  - **T2:** Unlocks resources up to level 60.
  - **T3:** Unlocks resources up to level 75.
  - **T4:** Unlocks resources up to level 90.
  - **T5:** Unlocks endgame resources up to level 120

## Gathering Commands

| Command | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| [[/island gather view]] | View current storage levels and progress across all camps. |
| [[/island gather preview]] | Preview uncollected resources. |
| [[/island gather collect]] | Collect resources from a specific camp. |
| [[/island gather collect_all]] | Collect all accumulated resources from all 5 camps in a single command. |
| [[/island gather lifetime]] | View your account's lifetime collected materials across all camps. |

## Gathering Camps Overview

### 1. Fishing Docks (Fishing)
- **Outputs:** Raw trout, Raw salmon, Raw lobster, Raw swordfish, Raw monkfish, Raw shark, Raw sea turtle, Raw manta ray, Raw rocktail.

### 2. Excavation Tunnels (Mining)
- **Outputs:** Copper ore, Tin ore, Iron ore, Coal, Mithril ore, Adamantite ore, Runite ore, Amethyst, Crystalline ore, Dense crystal shards.

### 3. Lumberyard (Woodcutting)
- **Outputs:** Normal logs, Oak logs, Willow logs, Teak logs, Maple logs, Mahogany logs, Yew logs, Magic logs, Redwood logs, Elder logs, Verdant logs, Living bark, Ancient verdant logs.

### 4. Divination Spire (Divination)
- **Outputs:** Pale energy, Flickering energy, Bright energy, Glowing energy, Sparkling energy, Gleaming energy, Vibrant energy, Lustrous energy, Brilliant energy, Radiant energy, Luminous energy, Incandescent energy, Ancient energy, Elder runes, Prismare.

### 5. Fertile Fields (Farming)
- **Crops & Herbs:** Potato, Onion, Cabbage, Tomato, Flax, Limpwurt root, Sweetcorn, Strawberry, Watermelon, Mort myre fungus, Snape grass, Ranarr weed, White berries, Snapdragon, Poison ivy berries, Dwarf weed, Dragon fruit, Torstol, Blood orange, Ignilace, Avocado, Spirit weed, Athelas, Mango, Grimy korulsi, Lychee, Advax berry, Crystal shard, Mystery box.
- **Seeds & Spores (including custom seeds):** Ivy seed, Blood orange seed, Brimstone spore, Ignilace seed, Avocado seed, Spirit weed seed, Athelas seed, Mysterious seed, Mango seed, Korulsi seed, Lychee seed, Advax berry seed, Grand crystal acorn.
