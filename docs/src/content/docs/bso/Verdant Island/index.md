The **Verdant Island** is a major endgame expansion featuring passive resource camps, facility progression, high-tier skilling, unique minigames, formidable dragon bosses, the massive **Burning Dominion** raid, and the elusive **Archon** megaboss.

---

## The Island Progression Loop

Progression on the Verdant Island is interconnected:

```
┌───────────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────────┐
│       Passive Camps       │ ---> │    Skilling Activities    │
│ (Docks, Tunnels, Forests) │      │(Mycology, Mining, Fishing)│
└─────────────┬─────────────┘      └─────────────┬─────────────┘
              │                                  │
              ▼                                  ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────────┐
│  Minigames & Refining     │ ---> │    Island Facilities      │
│ (Distillery, Contracts)   │      │(Warcamp, Sanctum, Conduit)│
└─────────────┬─────────────┘      └─────────────┬─────────────┘
              │                                  │
              ▼                                  ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Bosses & Burning Dominion │ ---> │   The Archon & BIS Gear   │
│(Twins, Sentinel, Behemoth)│      │ (Empyrean, Glaive, Aegis) │
└───────────────────────────┘      └───────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Harvest & Gather**: Gather raw materials via **Ancient Mycology**, **Archaic Mining**, **Gemstone Fishing**, or passive **Gathering Camps**.
2. **Refine & Distill**: Refine raw supplies into potions, elixirs, and contracts at the **Brimstone Distillery** and **Construction Contracts**.
3. **Upgrade Facilities**: Pool your own resources to construct and maintain the 12 **Island Facilities**
4. **Kill Bosses**: Slay the **Crystalline Sentinel**, **Fungal Behemoth**, **Orym**, **Orrodil**, and assemble a team for the **Burning Dominion** mass.
5. **Awaken the Archon & Make Endgame Gear**: Maintain the Archon Sanctum to roll Archon spawns after any island activity, unlocking **Empyrean Armour**.
