## Custom Content and Boosts

### Boosts

- [Herblore master cape](/bso/custom-items/capes/#master-capes) - 10% chance per potion made to receive an extra potion
- [Mecha Mortar](/bso/skills/invention/#inventions) - 45% speed boost to Herblore

There is no Herblore-boosting pet. [Herbert](/bso/custom-items/pets#meme-pets-and-no-perk-pets) is a perkless pet found while mixing potions.

### Heat res. potions

1. Create Athelas paste from crushing the Athelas herb: [[/mix name\:athelas paste]]
2. Craft Heat res. vials with molten glass and Athelas paste: [[/craft name\:heat res. vial]]
3. Mix a Super antifire and 4-dose brew or restore into the Heat res. vial, for example: [[/mix name\:heat res. brew]]

### Enhanced potions

1. Create Crystal dust from crystal shards: [[/create item\:crystal dust]]
2. Combine Korulsi and 10 Crystal dust with one base potion: Stamina, Restore, Brew, or Divine Water.

### Divination potion

1. Mix 1x Advax berry with 1x Spirit weed.
2. Grants variable extra energy/XP and a +10 level boost when harvesting memories. This allows you to harvest higher tiers without the required level.

### Other custom items

- Neem oil - made with Neem Drupe and Jug of Water.
- Dragon's fury - a potion made at 120 Herblore using 3 Ignecarus Scales and 1 Abyssal Dragon Bones.

### [Kibble](/bso/skills/cooking/#kibble)

Kibble is made with the [[/kibble]] command and used as Hunter bait or tame food.

- Simple Kibble
- Delicious Kibble
- Extraordinary Kibble

## Optimal Gear Setup

Herblore master cape, [Mecha Mortar](/bso/skills/invention/#inventions)
