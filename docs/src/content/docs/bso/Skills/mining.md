### Custom Content

- Dark Animica - Mined at 105 mining, used to create the Void Staff
- Obsidian - Mined at 80 mining, used to create volcanic items.


### Boosts

- [Doug](/bso/custom-items/pets) - gives 1.2x xp boost at Volcanic Mine
- [Dwarven Pickaxe](/bso/custom-items/#dwarven-equipment) - 1.5 tick speed mining, 3x boost to Volcanic Mine, requires 99 mining
- Volcanic pickaxe - 1.24 tick speed mining, requires 105 mining
- Offhand volcanic pickaxe - +150% quantity when mining, destroys all ores except obsidian while equipped, requires 100 Strength and 105 Mining - **This stacks with powermining**
- [Mining Master Cape](/bso/custom-items/capes/#master-capes) - 2x nuggets and minerals
- [Spiritual mining portent](/bso/skills/divination/#portents) - consumes stone spirits to grant extra mining XP, instead of extra ore
- Celestial ring - +4 invisible level boost


### Optimal Gear Setup

Volcanic pickaxe, Offhand volcanic pickaxe, Expert mining gloves, Prospectors outfit, Mining master cape
