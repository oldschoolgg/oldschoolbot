### **Custom Content and Boosts**

- [Dwarven Greataxe](/bso/custom-items/#dwarven-equipment) - 8x Woodcutting success multiplier
- [Drygore Axe](/bso/skills/invention/#inventions) - 10x Woodcutting success multiplier
- Elder Logs - logs obtained from Elder trees at level 105 Woodcutting
- [Woodcutting Master Cape](/bso/custom-items/capes/#master-capes) - 2x Woodcutting loot
- [Twitcher's Gloves](/osb/skills/woodcutting/forestry/#boosts) - `/chop name:Elder Logs twitchers_gloves:clue nest` - 20% increased chance to receive Clue Nests
- Forestry - `/chop name:Elder Logs forestry_events:True` - Gives additional XP in Construction, Hunter, Thieving, Woodcutting, Fletching, Farming (can be stacked with Twitcher's Gloves)
- Forestry Basket - +5 minute trip length
- Lumberjack outfit - 2.5% XP boost 

### Optimal Gear Setup

Full lumberjack outfit in skilling, Drygore Axe, Woodcutting master cape, Twitcher's gloves
